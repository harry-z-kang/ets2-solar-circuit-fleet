# Volvo, Iveco, MAN and Renault truck templates

Checked 26 November 2026. This reference covers **14 SCS truck models and 37 cabin layouts**, with **56 verified DDS templates** in total: 37 main cabin maps plus 19 auxiliary maps. All main cabin maps are **4096 × 4096**.

These are source templates for extending the fleet theme. They are not additional finished Solar Circuit paint jobs; the existing Scania, trailer and coach modules keep their current scope.

## Downloads

- **DDS templates:** [Download `templates.zip` directly from the author](https://github.com/Carsmaniac/paintjob-packer/releases/download/v2.1.3/templates.zip) — 351,533,456 bytes (about 335 MiB).
- **Paint Job Packer and release notes:** [v2.1.3](https://github.com/Carsmaniac/paintjob-packer/releases/tag/v2.1.3). The release specifically includes fixes for Volvo FH4, FH5 and FH6.
- **PNG / layered PSD templates:** [author’s SCS forum thread](https://forum.scssoft.com/viewtopic.php?f=33&t=272386) → Download / Mirror. The author’s general template listing describes its coverage as ETS2 1.59; use the v2.1.3 DDS archive for the exact files verified below.

For Paint Job Packer, place the unextracted `templates.zip` beside its executable and `.pck` file, as described on the release page. For direct editing, extract the model folders listed below. Ignore files beginning with `._`; they are macOS metadata, not usable textures.

## Using these for the fleet theme

1. Match the truck’s exact generation, cabin and chassis to the metadata and template. A brand name alone is not enough, particularly for Volvo FH6 Aero, MAN generations and 8x4 variants.
2. Lay out the reusable Solar Circuit artwork on that map, keeping text off glass, trim, vents and wheel arches. Some chassis variants share a cabin map but still need different accessory treatment.
3. Where an auxiliary template exists, paint it separately. Additional accessory groups in the vehicle metadata do not necessarily have their own wireframe file in the archive; inspect the generated definitions and use appropriate solid colors or verified mappings.
4. Hide wireframe layers before exporting the final DDS. Generate definitions for the exact vehicle with Paint Job Packer, replace its placeholder textures, and check both sides and all painted accessories in game.

The new templates have not been tested in the local ETS2 1.62.3.3 installation. Their availability and image integrity have been verified; exact UV compatibility with every current game variant still needs a game check. Third-party Volvo, Iveco, MAN and Renault truck mods may use different UVs and need their own author’s templates.
